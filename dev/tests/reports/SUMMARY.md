## Notes for owners (passing, but worth knowing)

- **HOME** `templates/index.json`: the five category blocks have no `collection`, so every Section E link is `/collections`
  (the merchant must bind them). Test 01 accepts this (contract only requires 5 links into `/collections`).
- **GSAP timing**: with `rootMargin: '100% 0px'`, GSAP is requested before the hero has been scrolled past (once the signature
  section is within one viewport). ARCH §4.5 (lazy margin) and §9 row 09 ("no GSAP before scrolling past the hero") conflict;
  test 09 asserts the unambiguous part (0 GSAP bytes before the first scroll; pin desktop-only; ≤ 10 ScrollTriggers).
- Chromium starts `loading="lazy"` images up to ~3 000 px below the viewport on an unknown connection type, so test 16 checks
  lazy images *beyond* that distance plus the `loading="lazy"` attribute on D/E/F imagery.
- Native modal `<dialog>` focus: Tab may pass through the browser chrome before wrapping; test 12 asserts focus never lands on
  page content behind the dialog.
- Axe (test 12) audits content at rest (`reducedMotion: 'reduce'`); while the featured-rail exposure lift is mid-animation
  axe reports card text at 1.05:1, which is a transient state.
